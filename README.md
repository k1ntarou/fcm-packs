# FCM packs

Optional downloadable content for **FCM: Football Club Mastermind** (Yero Games).
The game downloads these files from the in-game Editor; nothing here is bundled with the app.

- `packs.json` is the list of packs the game offers. Set `"enabled": false` on a pack to hide it in the game without an app update.
- `names/names.json` holds real player names for the clubs, keyed by the game's club id. Each player is `[first name, last name, position, date of birth, nationality code, rating]`.
  Since game 4.12 it also has `coaches` (`{club id: [name, nationality code, year of birth]}`) and `cyr`, the Russian and Ukrainian spelling of the names (`{word, "NAT:word" or full coach name: [ru, uk]}`); other languages show the names as written.
- `names/retro.zip` holds the squads and coaches of every season from 1998/99 to 2025/26 for careers with a historical start (game 4.12+): `YYYY.json` is the season YYYY/YY+1 (`clubs` and `coaches` as above) and `cyr.json` is the shared Russian/Ukrainian dictionary. Older seasons have fewer known players; the game makes up the rest. Since 2026-11-18.2 a retro row may have a 7th field: the player's career peak on the game's rating scale (from Transfermarkt market values, 0 or missing = unknown); game 4.12.1+ uses it for potential, older versions ignore it.
- `emblems/NN.zip` holds club emblems for league NN, one `<club id>.png` (256×256) per club. `emblems/hist.zip` holds the historic clubs of retro careers (ids 9001+), `emblems/old1-3.zip` the old clubs of the other leagues (ids 10000+, game 4.11+). `emblems/pol.zip` holds Polish I/II liga clubs (game 4.12.6+), `emblems/lower.zip` the new clubs of the six second divisions added in game 4.23 (England League One, Netherlands, Portugal, Turkey, Belgium, Scotland; ids 7600–8105), `emblems/lower2.zip` the new clubs of six more second divisions added in game 4.25 (Austria, Switzerland, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Czechia; ids 8200–8704, plus Östers IF). `emblems/eu5.zip` holds the clubs of five new European countries added in game 4.35 (Israel, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Slovenia; ids 520–525 and 15000–15408, plus their old clubs of retro seasons, ids 15800–16213). `emblems/eu5b.zip` holds the clubs of five more countries added in game 4.36 (Ireland, Finland, Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan, Georgia; ids 526–530 and 21000–21412, plus their old clubs of retro seasons, ids 16300–16711). `emblems/sa4.zip` holds the clubs of four South American countries added in game 4.37 (Peru, Paraguay, Bolivia, Venezuela; ids 539–551 and 21500–21810, plus their old clubs of retro seasons, ids 16800–17110). `emblems/as4.zip` holds the clubs of four Asian countries added in game 4.39 (Iran, Uzbekistan, Iraq, Thailand; ids 570–575 and 21900–22214, plus old clubs of retro seasons 17300, 17301, 17401). `emblems/na5.zip` holds the clubs of five North and Central American countries added in game 4.43 (Canada, Costa Rica, Honduras, Guatemala, Panama; ids 553–560 and 22300–22710, plus the old club 17900). `emblems/af5.zip` holds the clubs of five African countries added in game 4.45 (Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia, Algeria, South Africa; ids 22800–23215, plus old clubs of retro seasons 18100–18408 and the CAF Champions League guests 578–581). `emblems/sa2.zip` holds the new clubs of the Brazilian Série B and the Argentine Primera Nacional, second divisions added in game 4.46 (ids 23300–23306 and 23400–23419, plus the historic clubs 11810 and 11915, which had no emblem yet). `emblems/sea4.zip` holds the clubs of four Asian countries added in game 4.48 (India, Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia; ids 23500–23811, plus old clubs of retro seasons 18805–19012).

Player names and club emblems belong to their owners. If you own content here and want it removed, open an issue and it will be taken down.
